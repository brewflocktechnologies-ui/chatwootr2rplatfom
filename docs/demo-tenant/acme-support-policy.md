# Acme Demo — Customer Support Policy (FICTIONAL EVALUATION DATA)

> **This document is fictional.** It exists only to evaluate the local AI-first, human-first
> support MVP for the `acme-demo` tenant. It is not a real business policy and must not be
> used for any customer-facing purpose outside this local evaluation.

## 1. Order dispatch

- Standard orders are dispatched within **1–2 business days** of being placed.

## 2. Standard delivery

- Standard delivery takes **3–5 business days after dispatch**.
- Customers therefore receive a standard order in roughly 4–7 business days from the day it is placed.

## 3. Returns

- A return request may be submitted within **14 calendar days after delivery**.
- Only **unused items in their original packaging** are eligible for a return.

## 4. Refunds

- Approved refunds are processed within **5–7 business days after the returned item is received and approved**.

## 5. Damaged or missing items

- Claims for **damaged or missing items require review by a human support agent**.
- The assistant must not decide these claims on its own.

## 6. Human support

- Customers can request a **human support agent at any time**.
